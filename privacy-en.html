1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information. All settings you configure (colors, style, size, position, and so on) are kept only on your device.

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. In order to serve and measure those ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes certain data automatically, as described in section 2.

2. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

The App uses the following third-party SDKs provided by Google LLC:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) – used to display banner ads in the App.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) – used to request and manage your consent for advertising where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland).

For ad delivery and analytics purposes, these SDKs collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID (AD_ID) and the app set ID;
  • IP address and the approximate (non-precise) location derived from it;
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were requested, displayed, viewed or clicked) and diagnostic data.

This data is used for ad delivery and analytics (including personalized ads where you have given your consent), to measure ad performance, to detect fraud and abuse, and to improve Google's advertising services. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

Where required by law, the App asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the App (About → Privacy options), or by contacting us at boybeak@gmail.com. For more information about how Google handles this data, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Ads and how to opt out of personalized advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads), or manage personalized ads through Google Ad Settings (https://adssettings.google.com).

3. How We Use Your Information

We do not use your personal information ourselves. Data processed by the advertising SDKs is used only as described in section 2.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. To provide and measure advertising, the Google Mobile Ads SDK integrated in the App shares the advertising-related data described in section 2 (such as your advertising ID, device information and ad interaction data) with Google LLC, which processes it in accordance with its own privacy policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not require an account, so there is no account data to delete. Your settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Advertising data collected by the SDKs is retained by Google in accordance with Google's privacy policy, and you can limit its use through the advertising ID settings mentioned in section 2.
